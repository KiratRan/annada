## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp fajita seasoning

## Instructions

1. Toss chicken and vegetables with seasoning on a sheet pan.
2. Roast at 425°F for 25 minutes.
3. Serve with tortillas.
