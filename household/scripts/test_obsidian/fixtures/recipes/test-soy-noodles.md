## Ingredients

- 200 g wheat noodles
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1 green onion, sliced

## Instructions

1. Cook noodles; drain.
2. Stir-fry carrot and green onion briefly.
3. Toss noodles with soy sauce and vegetables over high heat.
