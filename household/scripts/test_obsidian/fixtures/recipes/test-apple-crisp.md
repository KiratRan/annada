## Ingredients

- 4 apples, peeled and sliced
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/3 cup flour
- 1/4 cup brown sugar

## Instructions

1. Arrange apples in a baking dish.
2. Mix oats, flour, and sugar; sprinkle over fruit.
3. Bake at 350°F for 40 minutes until golden.
