## Ingredients

- 1 cup red lentils
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp ghee

## Instructions

1. Rinse lentils and simmer in 3 cups water for 25 minutes.
2. Temper spices in ghee; stir into dal.
3. Serve hot with rice.
