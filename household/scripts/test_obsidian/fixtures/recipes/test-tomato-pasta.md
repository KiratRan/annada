## Ingredients

- 250 g penne pasta
- 2 cups crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 clove garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta until al dente; drain.
2. Simmer crushed tomatoes and garlic for 10 minutes.
3. Stir in cream, toss with pasta, and serve.
